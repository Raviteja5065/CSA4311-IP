About the Journal

IJSSE aims to encourage the exchange of sound scholarly research in scientific and social disciplines. The journal supports responsible research, constructive peer review, and accessible communication of published work.

Mission

To provide a fair, professional, and transparent route for research from submission through peer review and publication.

Scope

The journal welcomes original work and reviews across scientific, social, interdisciplinary, and applied research areas.

Publication Ethics

Authors, reviewers, and editors are expected to uphold research integrity, declare relevant conflicts of interest, protect confidential material, and avoid plagiarism, duplicate submission, and fabricated or falsified results. Concerns should be reported to the editorial office for fair investigation.
