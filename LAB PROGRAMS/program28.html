Why join us?

Ideas are stronger when we share them.

The Community Innovation Forum brings together local leaders, students, nonprofit teams, educators, entrepreneurs, and anyone interested in making their community a better place to live. Attendees will learn from real projects, exchange approaches, and leave with ideas they can put into practice.
